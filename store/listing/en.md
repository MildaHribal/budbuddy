## Full description (max 4000)

BudBuddy is a journal for home growers. You log each plant from germination through flowering, harvest, drying and curing, and the history stays readable when you look back at a finished grow.

The journal works offline and without an account. Plants, entries and photos are stored on your device.

JOURNAL
• Stages: germination, seedling, vegetative, flowering, harvest (wet weight), drying, curing (dry weight), archive
• Entries: watering, feeding (ml, nutrients, pH, EC), training (topping, LST, SCROG and more), pest treatment, measurements (height, temperature, humidity), notes and photos
• Watering intervals and tasks, with a "Today" list on the home screen
• Photo gallery and per-plant timeline
• Statistics: logging streak, activity, water and feed totals

TOOLS
• Nutrient mixer that scales a per-litre dose to your watering can
• VPD calculator
• DLI / PPFD calculator for your light schedule
• Unit converters

GUIDE
Short lessons that follow a grow from seed to jar. Read them in any order; the guide can hide lessons that do not apply to your setup.

ANDROID
• Local reminders for watering and tasks at a time you choose
• Home-screen widgets: light cycle, plants, latest photo, AI shortcut
• Backup to a JSON file and restore on another device

OPTIONAL, ONLINE
• AI assistant: ask about a plant, optionally with a photo. Requires signing in with Google and has a daily limit. The question, the photo and a summary of that plant's journal are sent through our server to an AI model (Google Gemini) to generate the answer. Questions and photos are not stored on our side, and you can report any answer.
• Community: questions, tips, public plants, strain grow reports and monthly photo challenges. Requires signing in with Google. You can report content and block users, and you can delete your account in Settings.

No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs.

BudBuddy is for adults (18+). It does not sell or help buy anything. Grow only where it is legal for you to do so. Information in the app, including AI answers, is not professional advice.
